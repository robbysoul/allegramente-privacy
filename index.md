## English

### Introduction

AllegraMente ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application AllegraMente on iOS.

### Information We Collect

#### Information Stored Locally on Your Device

AllegraMente stores all your data **locally on your device only**, using Apple's SwiftData framework and device storage (UserDefaults). This includes:

- User profile information (display name, avatar preference, theme/gradient)
- Workout history and scores
- Achievement progress
- Weekly goal settings and history
- Onboarding assessment results (self-reported memory preferences and goals)
- Training program enrollment and progress (active program, completed weeks, session difficulty)
- Notification preferences (enabled/disabled, time, frequency)
- General app preferences

**We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers.**

#### Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect personal identification information
- We do not track your location
- We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
- We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs
- We do not share any data with third parties
- We do not use advertising networks

### Notifications

AllegraMente may request permission to send you **local notifications** (e.g., workout reminders). These notifications are generated entirely on your device — no data is sent to any server to deliver them. You can enable or disable notifications at any time from the app's Settings screen or from your device's iOS Settings.

### In-App Purchases

If you choose to support the app through optional in-app purchases, transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or any data related to the purchase beyond confirmation that it was completed.

### Data Storage and Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. Your data:

- Is never transmitted to any server
- Is not backed up to iCloud (unless you enable iCloud backup at the device level)
- Is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app
- Can be deleted per-profile from within the app at any time

### Children's Privacy

AllegraMente is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13. Since all data is stored locally on the device and we do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, the app fully complies with COPPA and similar regulations.

### Security

Since all data remains on your device and is never transmitted, the security of your data depends on the security of your device. We recommend keeping your device and iOS up to date.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

### Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact:
**support@allegramente.app**
